Bio

Who I am and why I build

Hey, I’m Kayden — a high school student and web developer based in Lethbridge, Alberta. I build websites and small projects using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and some beginner Python. Most of what I know came from just trying things out, breaking them, and figuring out how to fix them. I usually learn by doing. That means a lot of experimenting, problem-solving, and improving things as I go. I like building things that actually work well and are easy to use, not just stuff that looks good on the surface. Outside of coding, I’m into tech, city-building games, woodworking, and thinking through business or side project ideas. Right now I’m focused on improving my skills and building up a portfolio that shows what I can actually do, not just what I’ve learned.
