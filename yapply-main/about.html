Bio

Who I am and why I build

Hey, I’m Kayden — a high school student and web developer based in Lethbridge, Alberta. I build websites and small projects using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and beginner-level Python. Most of what I know came from experimenting, breaking things, and figuring out how to fix them. I learn best by doing, which means a lot of problem-solving, testing ideas, and improving projects as I go. I enjoy building things that are not only visually appealing but also practical, responsive, and easy to use. Outside of coding, I’m interested in technology, city-building games, woodworking, and exploring business or side-project ideas. Right now, I’m focused on continuing to improve my development skills and building a portfolio that demonstrates what I can create through real projects and hands-on experience.
